# Notely FAQ

## What is Notely?

Notely is a note-taking app: notes in Markdown, notebooks, tags, attachments
and full-text search, synced across the web, desktop and mobile apps. Teams
use shared spaces to write documents together. There are three plans: Free,
Pro and Team (see "Plans and billing").

## Which platforms does Notely run on?

The web app at app.notely.app in any current browser; desktop apps for
macOS 12+, Windows 10+ and Linux (AppImage, .deb); mobile apps for iOS 16+
and Android 10+. All of them use the same account and the same notes.

## I can't sign in. What should I check first?

Look at the message the sign-in page shows, then at the matching error code
in "Signing in to Notely". The most frequent causes are a wrong password
(WRONG_PASSWORD), a lock after too many attempts (ACCOUNT_LOCKED), an email
that was never confirmed (EMAIL_NOT_VERIFIED), a company that switched to
single sign-on (SSO_REQUIRED) and two-factor codes from a phone with a wrong
clock (INVALID_2FA_CODE). When you write to support, add the email address
of the account: support can then see the account's sign-in history.

## I forgot my password

Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 1
hour. Accounts created with "Continue with Google" have no password: use the
Google button. Accounts on an SSO domain sign in through their company.

## How do I contact support?

Email support@notely.app or use the chat in **Help → Contact support**. Write
from the account's email address and describe what you see, with the exact
message or error code. Response times depend on the plan: within 2 business
days on Free, 4 hours on Pro and 1 hour on Team.

## Is my data private?

Notes are encrypted at rest and in transit and stored in the EU. Notely staff
can't read note contents. See "Account and data".
