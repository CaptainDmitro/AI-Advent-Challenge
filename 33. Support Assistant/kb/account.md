# Account and data

## Changing the email address

Open **Settings → Account → Email**, enter the new address and confirm it
with the link Notely sends there. Until it is confirmed, the old address
keeps working. Accounts on an SSO domain can't change their email: the
workspace admin manages it.

## Exporting notes

**Settings → Account → Export** creates a ZIP archive with every note as a
Markdown file, the attachments in their original format, and the notebooks
as folders. A link to the archive is sent by email when it is ready, usually
within 10 minutes, and is valid for 7 days. Team admins can export the whole
workspace from the admin console.

## Deleting the account

**Settings → Account → Delete account** schedules the deletion. The account
can be restored by signing in within 30 days. After that, the notes and
attachments are deleted for good. An active subscription is cancelled with no
refund for the remaining period.

## Where the data is stored

Notes are stored in data centres in the EU (Frankfurt) and encrypted at
rest and in transit. Notely staff can't read note contents. Support sees the
account's profile, plan, security settings and sign-in history, but not the
notes.
