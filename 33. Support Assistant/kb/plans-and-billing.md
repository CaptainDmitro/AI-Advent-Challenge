# Plans and billing

## Plans

| | Free | Pro | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $6 per month, or $60 per year | $10 per member per month |
| Devices with sync | 2 | unlimited | unlimited |
| Storage | 1 GB | 50 GB | 100 GB per member |
| Note history | 7 days | 1 year | 1 year |
| Shared spaces | — | 3 | unlimited |
| Two-factor authentication | yes | yes | yes, can be required by the admin |
| Single sign-on (SSO) | — | — | yes |
| Support | email, 2 business days | email and chat, within 4 hours | priority, within 1 hour |

## Changing the plan

Upgrade or downgrade in **Settings → Plan**. An upgrade starts at once and
the price is prorated for the rest of the billing period. A downgrade takes
effect at the end of the paid period. On a downgrade to Free, devices beyond
the first two stop syncing (see "Sync"), but nothing is deleted.

## Payments and invoices

Notely accepts cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and, for Team
yearly plans, bank transfer. Invoices are in **Settings → Billing →
Invoices**, as PDF, with the company name and VAT number if they were
entered before the payment. Invoices can't be changed after they are issued.

## Failed payments

When a payment fails, Notely retries it after 3 and 7 days and sends an email
each time. After 14 days without payment the account is suspended (see
"Signing in" → ACCOUNT_SUSPENDED). Updating the card in the billing portal
retries the payment at once and lifts the suspension.

## Refunds

A new Pro or Team subscription can be refunded in full within 14 days of the
first payment. Ask support from the account's email address. Renewals are not
refunded, but the subscription can be cancelled at any time and stays active
until the end of the paid period.
